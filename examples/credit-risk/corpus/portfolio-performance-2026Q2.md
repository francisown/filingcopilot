# Retail Portfolio Performance — 2026 Q2

**Document control:** Synthetic demo data. Reporting date: 2026-06-30. Currency: SGD. Classification: internal demo data. These figures are fabricated for a retrieval demonstration and are not a real portfolio.

## 1. Portfolio summary

The retail portfolio contains 48,200 accounts with S$1.42 billion of exposure. The overall 30+ DPD rate is 2.3%, up 0.2 percentage points from 2026 Q1. The overall 90+ DPD rate is 0.8%. Coverage ratio is 112%. Net charge-off for the quarter was S$8.4 million.

The near-prime unsecured segment has the highest 30+ DPD rate at 4.8%. It also has the lowest coverage ratio at 98%. Prime unsecured is the lowest-risk unsecured segment at 1.2% 30+ DPD. Mortgage has the lowest 30+ DPD rate overall at 0.9%.

## 3. Quarter-on-quarter movement

Near-prime 30+ DPD rose from 4.2% in 2026 Q1 to 4.8% in 2026 Q2, an increase of 0.6 percentage points. Prime unsecured rose from 1.1% to 1.2%. Auto was flat at 2.1%. Mortgage fell from 1.0% to 0.9%. The near-prime deterioration is consistent with the calibration finding in the 2026 RetailScore validation report.

## 4. Early-warning indicators

Applications with two or more bureau inquiries in the last six months show a 30+ DPD rate of 7.4%, compared with 2.0% for applicants with no recent inquiries. The near-prime segment accounts for 62% of the recent-inquiry population. This overlap is the first diagnostic to review in the next credit policy cycle.

## 5. Data notes

The portfolio figures are synthetic. Segment definitions follow the credit policy. DPD is measured at month end. Exposure is outstanding principal before provisions. Coverage ratio is the allowance for credit losses divided by 90+ DPD exposure. No customer-level data is included in this document.
