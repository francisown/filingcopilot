# Independent review protocol

The current evaluation is self-reviewed. This protocol defines the minimum
review needed before any claim of human relevance or financial correctness.

## Sample

- Draw from delivered answers, rejected drafts, refusals, and numeric-only
  answers, not only successful claims.
- Stratify by question type: metrics-generated, domain-relevant, novel-generated,
  annual comparison, risk/qualitative, and refusal.
- Minimum target: 60 questions, with at least 10 from each stratum; report the
  actual distribution.
- Reviewers see the question, answer, cited evidence, and package; they do not
  see the model name or the author's intended verdict.

## Questions

For each answer, record separately:

1. Is the answer supported by the cited evidence?
2. Is the period, unit, currency, and subject correct?
3. Is a calculated number arithmetically correct from the cited inputs?
4. Are material assumptions and missing evidence disclosed?
5. Is the answer overconfident, misleading, or an investment recommendation?
6. Would the reviewer trust this as a research draft? (yes / with corrections / no)

Disagreement is resolved by a third reviewer or retained as a reported
disagreement; do not average it away.

## Reporting

- Report raw counts and 95% intervals, not only percentages.
- Report reviewer identity class (domain analyst, auditor, engineer) without
  publishing personal data.
- Publish false-support examples, not only aggregate scores.
- Keep self-review and independent review in separate columns.
